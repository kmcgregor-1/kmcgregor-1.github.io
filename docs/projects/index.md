# Projects

<p class="page-lede">Research projects in transient astrophysics, survey science, and statistical inference.</p>

    <div class="project-copy">
      <p class="project-number">01 · Survey selection</p>
      <h2 id="frb-survey-selection">Measuring observational bias in real-time FRB surveys</h2>
      <p>Triggered surveys <strong>do not observe every burst equally</strong>. I model how detection thresholds, real-time triggering, and instrumental sensitivity transform an <strong>intrinsic FRB population</strong> into an observed sample.</p>
      <p class="project-outcome"><strong>Goal</strong> Build population analyses that carry survey selection effects through to the final inference.</p>

  <article class="project-card project-card--reverse">
    <div class="project-copy">
      <p class="project-number">02 · FRB populations</p>
      <h2>Unifying repeating and apparent non-repeating FRBs</h2>
      <p>Some FRB sources repeat while many have been observed only once. This project tests whether those groups can arise from a <strong>shared underlying population</strong> after accounting for observing cadence, source activity, and the probability of detecting another burst.</p>
      <p class="project-outcome"><strong>Question</strong> When does a source appear to be a one-off because of its physics, and when because of how it was observed?</p>
    </div>

    </figure>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <div class="project-copy">
      <p class="project-number">03 · Early-Universe constraints</p>
      <h2>Forecasting cosmic-string limits with radio transient surveys</h2>
      <p>Radio transient surveys can test <strong>models of the early Universe</strong>. I develop forecasts for how next-generation facilities may <strong>constrain cosmic-string populations</strong> through their predicted transient signatures.</p>
      <p class="project-outcome"><strong>Approach</strong> Translate survey design and null results into robust upper limits on cosmic-string models.</p>
