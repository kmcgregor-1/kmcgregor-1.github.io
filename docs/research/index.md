# Research & Publications

<p class="page-lede">I study fast radio burst populations with methods designed for the realities of modern, real-time astronomical surveys.</p>

Within the CHIME/FRB Collaboration, I use population models, simulations, and **Bayesian inference** to study the sources and environments of fast radio bursts. By accounting for **how survey selection effects shape the bursts we detect**, I connect CHIME/FRB's growing sample to the underlying astrophysical population.
